# Volatile Data

The fastest way for this skill to cause real damage is a confidently stated exam date that moved, or an eligibility rule that changed. A student can lose a year to one wrong sentence.

So the design rule is simple: **structure is remembered, numbers are verified.**

## Never state from memory

Treat every one of these as unknown until confirmed from an official source in the current session:

- Exam dates, registration windows, and correction windows
- Cutoffs, closing ranks, qualifying marks, category-wise cutoffs
- Fees — tuition, hostel, exam, application
- Eligibility rules: minimum percentages, subject combinations, age limits, attempt limits, domicile and reservation rules
- Seat counts, intake, new institutions, new campuses
- Salary and placement figures
- Visa, post-study work and immigration rules for any country
- Scholarship amounts, criteria and deadlines
- Anything introduced by a new education policy rollout, which lands at different times in different states

## Safe to state from structure

- The existence and general purpose of an exam or route
- The broad sequence of a path — exam, then counselling, then admission
- The shape of a decision and its trade-offs
- Typical duration of a degree or qualification
- Which authority governs a route, and therefore whose website is authoritative

## How to handle it in the output

If a live source is available, check it and cite the date checked.

If it is not available, do not omit the option and do not guess. Write it as an explicit gap:

> **CUET-UG registration** — typically opens early in the calendar year, but the exact window changes annually. Confirm at nta.ac.in before relying on this. *(Not verified — checked nothing live in this session.)*

Then list it again under **What I could not verify** at the end of the document, with the official page to check.

This section is a feature, not an apology. A student who knows which three things to verify is better served than one handed twenty confident figures, four of which are a year out of date.

## Authoritative sources

Point students at the source of truth, never at an aggregator or a coaching-site listicle:

| Route | Authority |
|---|---|
| JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET, UGC NET | nta.ac.in and the exam-specific portals |
| JEE Advanced | the hosting IIT's portal for that year |
| State CETs | the state technical education or examination board |
| GATE | the hosting IIT/IISc for that year |
| CAT | the hosting IIM for that year |
| UPSC | upsc.gov.in |
| SSC, Railways, Banking | ssc.gov.in, rrbapply.gov.in, ibps.in |
| CA / CS / CMA | icai.org, icsi.edu, icmai.in |
| Medical seats and rules | nmc.org.in, mcc.nic.in |
| Nursing, AYUSH | the respective national commission |
| Study abroad | the destination country's official immigration site |

A useful habit to pass on to the student: for anything that decides a year of their life, read the official notification PDF itself, not a summary of it.
